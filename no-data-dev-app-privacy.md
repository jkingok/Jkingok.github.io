## Overview
This privacy policy outlines how apps indicated as collecting no data
behave with your data.

## Information Collection
These apps are not intended to collect your data.

These apps are however currently in development. That means the app platform
may collect your data as a result of diagnostics - such as via a crash report.

You retain the ability to reject the sending of crash reports through the prompt
presented and the settings within the TestFlight app.

Any data collected will be transmitted to the developer securely via Apple and
will be kept temporarily only for the purposes of diagnosing faults and improving the app.

### Place Compare
Place Compare is a location comparison app.

It does not ask for your current location and does not track you.

It stores the information you enter locally on your device. The locations you select and
the places of interest you elect are searched for using Apple Maps and will be associated
in accordance with the Apple Maps privacy handling, especially once you leave the app to
view a location.

If you share a location from Google Maps into the app, then the Google server may be contacted
to attempt to decipher the location.

Any searches you perform a cached in a local database which may be synced into your iCloud Backup
if enabled.

## Third-Party Services
There are no third party services with your data at this time.

You will be able to check through the notices of TestFlight for any change.

If you choose to manually report an issue, then GitHub (Microsoft) will have that information.

GitHub Pages hosts this website. I do not have any analytics on it.

## Contact
If you have any questions regarding this policy, please reach out via the standard means for this site
which is currently GitHub Discussions, or via the details in the TestFlight app.
