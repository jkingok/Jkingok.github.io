Okay so “finally” you might say I walked into a paid AI coding experience.  
  
This is *not a sponsored post* because I definitely just paid $34 for access to **Claude Code.**  
  
This was a bit of a leap for me. I expected to be underwhelmed and I was wrong. I expected to be facing a new level of AI addiction or “getting my money’s worth” and I was right.  
  
So: Yes. Claude Code has been trained  *particularly well*. It’s out of the box capabilities are excellent and it’s very well suited to the Python coding tasks I’m giving it as you expect.  
  
And I’m constantly rationing and watching the usage meter because it is excellent at test-driven-development (which really plays into the hands of otherwise-flaky AI temperature-based tool use that I’d seen on some open models). A big surprise to me was the built-in integration of the iOS Simulator - it kind of led me into that without much asking.  
  
So I **am very concerned** about security with this. There is *no way* yet that I am giving it “sensitive” work. I’m only giving it open source development, and I **do want to up my sandboxing game** here but for now I’m trusting the Anthropic guardrails (and reputation damage), and in terms of accidental deletion well… it’s only my entire iCloud Drive and Passwords and… (oh goodness what *isn’t* accessible from this MacBook Pro?)  
  
But it’s enabled me to do things that were taking me weeks and liberated me back to developing with just my iPhone once again (I do have to check into the MacBook Pro every so often).  
  
My [MyPyStub](https://github.com/jkingok/mypystub) TestFlight app for Python development integrates really well with Claude, because it can be instructed *in the chat of the mobile app* to generate compatible test scripts and the .py file is copyable or saveable directly into my app, so easy development here we come! And I’ve been directing the development of features from my Wishlist all week.  
  
So overall I see the pros and cons and the change. What I really struggle with and so I expect to see more development (if only they would stop buying up all the supply) of racked local AI for business with model subscriptions as the way forward because we seriously don’t expect businesses to be consciously handing over all of their knowledge and IP and PII do we? *Listen to the mathematicians and their recent experiences - you might not have a business if the AI (companies) sees it can take over.*  
🧠
