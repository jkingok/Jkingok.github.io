It’s possible in my *Claude-*related fervour that I failed to keep my blog apprised of progress.  
  
**MyPyStub** was *soft-launched* onto the BeeWare Discord a couple of weeks ago when I finally dared to push “the button” on the *Apple Beta App Review* process.  
  
I was then *immediately (sigh!)* asked for an Android version and why it didn’t work on Windows.  
  
So the last couple of weeks have been **fun!**  
  
In any case, there’s now a version that can be tested *everywhere** (your exact mileage will vary), though the value of it on desktop is limited.  
  
The iOS version, which is the one that I use (and therefore still gets features first) is **on TestFlight.**  
  
TestFlight has two tiers of membership - internal and external.   
  
I recommend you start on the **External** group. Those builds lag slightly because they need to go through *Apple Beta App Review* so I’ll probably do that on somewhat stable builds weekly or so.  
  
[Join the MyPyStub beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/Px1Gkb4k) on iOS  
  
However if you really get excited then you’ll want to be an **Internal** tester which means you need to reach out to me with your Apple ID. (I suggest you use Send Beta App Feedback.)  
  
The Android version, for which I already have 1 tester (thank you!) is via the **Firebase App Tester** app, which means you will need **sideloading** capability on your device. (I do not have the account necessary for Google Play yet.)  
  
[Join the MyPyStub beta](https://appdistribution.firebase.dev/i/9ed9aa7d815d4474) on Android  
  
The Android version is *also* available in an alternative variant. There is a legacy of users with packages that aren’t ready to work on the latest Python 3.15. For those users I have a Python 3.13 build on **GitHub** at [Releases](https://github.com/jkingok/mypystub/releases). There is no capability without side loading or creating a fully separate app for this on iOS so without any demand it won’t exist.  
  
Anyone who wants to use this on desktop or make their own build can find the source code and all of the deliciously AI-generated instructions on [GitHub](https://github.com/jkingok/mypystub) which is also where you can file [Issues](https://github.com/jkingok/mypystub/issues).  
  
A new version out *right now* (coming soon-ish to TestFlight - that’s out of my hands).  
  
**Note that I promise no stability in this tool, it’s one major reason why you’ll find I use date-based versioning, no one build is better than the other.** *However Claude has as I pointed out already built thousands of tests…*  
🤞
